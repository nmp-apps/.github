## 🧭 About NMP

NMP (No More Problem) is an open-source initiative focused on simplifying and enhancing daily system interactions on Linux desktop environments. Inspired by the user experience of Windows and macOS, and the modular flexibility of Linux.

---

## 💡 Why "No More Problem"?

We believe interacting with your system shouldn't be frustrating.
NMP is built to eliminate the everyday pain points and replace them with confidence, clarity, and control.
